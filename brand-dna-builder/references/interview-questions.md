# Brand DNA Interview Questions

Use these questions to fill gaps the website couldn't answer. Ask in batches of 3-4 at a time — don't dump the full list on the user.

Prioritize the Essential questions first. Ask Optional questions only if the user is engaged and wants to build a thorough brand DNA.

## Essential Questions

### Target Customer

1. **Who's your primary customer?** Describe them in 2-3 sentences — age range, gender, lifestyle, what they do for work, what they care about.

2. **What specific problem does your product solve for them?** Be concrete. Not "feel better" — what exactly is broken in their life that your product fixes?

3. **What's the #1 reason they buy from you instead of a competitor?** Price? Quality? Ingredients? Mission? Results?

### Brand Voice Intent

4. **How do you want your brand to sound?** Pick 3-5 words. Examples: confident, warm, playful, authoritative, honest, edgy, premium, approachable.

5. **Are there any words or phrases you always use?** Proprietary terms, catchphrases, signature language.

6. **Are there any words, tones, or claims you NEVER want in your copy?** Things that feel off-brand or that you've ruled out for legal/ethical reasons.

### Positioning

7. **Who are your top 2-3 competitors?** Direct competitors customers would consider alongside you.

8. **What do you do better than them?** Specific, not vague. "Higher quality ingredients" is vague. "Only brand using clinically-tested concentrations of X" is specific.

9. **What's the one thing you want customers to remember about your brand after they leave the site?**

## Optional Questions (for deeper brand DNA)

### Priority Products

10. **Which products drive the majority of your revenue?** (Top 2-3 SKUs)

11. **Which product is easiest to sell to a new customer?** (Best gateway product)

12. **Are there any products you're actively pushing hard right now?** (Launches, hero products, seasonal focus)

### Customer Insights

13. **What does your best customer look like?** (LTV, purchase frequency, subscription status, referral behavior)

14. **What's the most common objection you hear before purchase?**

15. **What's the most common reason customers come back?**

### Creative Context

16. **Do you have brand guidelines I should reference?** (Optional — if they have a PDF brand guide, this is useful)

17. **Are there any creative directions you've tried that didn't work?** (Saves time — don't repeat failed experiments)

18. **Are there any ad hooks, angles, or campaigns that have performed exceptionally well?** (These become reference patterns for future creative)

### Compliance

19. **Are you in a regulated category?** (Supplements, CBD, health claims, financial, etc. — affects ad copy rules)

20. **Any legal disclaimers or approved claim language we need to include in ads?**

## Agency-Specific Questions

If the user is an agency setting up a client, also ask:

- **Who's your main point of contact at the brand?** (For approvals and creative direction)
- **What's the review/approval process before ads go live?** (Single reviewer? Team? Legal?)
- **Are there existing creative assets we should inventory?** (Brand assets folder, DAM, Google Drive)
- **What KPIs is the brand optimizing for?** (ROAS target, CPA target, scale goals)

## Question Ordering

Ask in this order when possible:

**Batch 1 (Essential):** Target customer (Q1-3)
**Batch 2 (Essential):** Brand voice intent (Q4-6)
**Batch 3 (Essential):** Positioning (Q7-9)
**Batch 4 (Optional):** Priority products (Q10-12)
**Batch 5 (Optional):** Customer insights (Q13-15)
**Batch 6 (Optional):** Creative context (Q16-18)
**Batch 7 (Optional):** Compliance (Q19-20)

Most brands only need Batches 1-3. Stop there unless the user wants to go deeper.

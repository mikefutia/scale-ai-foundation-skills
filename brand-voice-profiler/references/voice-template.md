## Template Rules

1. **Every rule must have a real quoted example.** No abstract descriptions without proof.
2. **Do/Don't word lists come from real copy or explicit user input.** Never invent words the brand "might" use.
3. **The Voice Summary at the top is the most important field.** A downstream copy skill should be able to read just that paragraph and start writing in the voice.
4. **Calibration Examples section is required.** It shows the voice in action and records the user's actual preferences.
5. **How to Write a Headline and How to Write a Body Paragraph are prescriptive.** These are the quick-reference rules downstream skills use as shortcuts.
